# Human Mind & Body Skills for Claude

Two evidence-based Claude Skills that make Claude more accurate, structured, and practical when explaining how people think, feel, decide, and how the human body works.

## Skills

### human-psychology
Motivation, decisions, habits, game psychology, persuasion, emotion, memory, the mind part by part, manipulation (how it works, how to spot and counter it), and ethics.
- Evidence grades (A-D) on every claim, including famous findings that failed to replicate
- 11 fill-in templates (behavior diagnosis, game retention audit, decision-stall audit, onboarding, habit plan, negotiation prep, ethics audit, manipulation analysis and more)
- Worked example reports and an exam engine with answer keys

### human-physiology
Graduate-level physiology of every organ system: cells, nerves, muscle and exercise, heart, lungs, kidneys and acid-base, gut and metabolism, hormones, blood and clotting, immunity, reproduction and aging, plus multi-system scenarios (shock, sepsis, dehydration, DKA).
- Key equations, normal values, and feedback loops
- Exam engine: blueprint, item templates, case and calculation formats, 20 sample questions with keys

## Install
1. Download `human-psychology.skill` and/or `human-physiology.skill` from the `dist` folder (or zip either skill folder yourself).
2. In Claude, open Settings > Capabilities > Skills and upload the file.

## Example: with vs without the skill
**Question:** "How do I make my game more engaging?"
- **Without:** a solid list of tactics (loops, rewards, streaks, social features).
- **With:** tactics organized around the three needs that best predict long-term play (competence, autonomy, relatedness), each graded by evidence, myths corrected (habits take ~66 days on average, not 21), a ranked action plan, metrics to track, and an ethics check.

## Ethics
The psychology skill explains manipulation tactics in depth for recognition, defense, teaching, research, and fiction, and steers influence advice toward designs people would endorse if they understood them.
